The War That Putin Won

Jul 25, 2026 · 16 min · 62 sources

The Caucasus. August 2008.

The world is focused on the Beijing Olympics. But in the mountains of the Caucasus, something else is happening, something most of the world barely notices. Russian tanks are rolling into Georgia.

The war lasts just five days. But in those five days, Russia shows the world something. A preview of what's to come. A rehearsal.

This is the story of the Russo-Georgian War.
