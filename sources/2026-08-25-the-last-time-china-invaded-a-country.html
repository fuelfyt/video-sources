The Last Time China Invaded a Country

Aug 25, 2026 · 18 min · 10 sources

The Sino-Vietnamese border. February 17, 1979.

Just before dawn. In the cold and the dark, hundreds of thousands of Chinese troops begin to move. Artillery opens fire. Tanks roll forward. Infantry advances across the frontier from Yunnan in the west to Guangxi in the east.

This is the last time China launches a large-scale invasion of another country.
